Privacy policy

How the information you choose to share when contacting Next Generation Tour is used.

Data controller and contact

This website presents the NGT — Next Generation Tour brand. For privacy questions, data rights, or details about the project operator, contact info@nextgenerationtour.es. The operator’s complete legal identity must be verified with the business owner before this notice can be considered final.

Data and purposes

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, enquiry category and message. These details are used to process and respond to your enquiry, not for default marketing or sale of contact databases. Please do not include sensitive information.

Legal basis

Depending on the enquiry, processing is based on steps requested before entering into a contract or the legitimate interest in replying to messages received. Additional information will be given if further processing is required.

External services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts. FormSubmit, an external form service, processes form submissions and delivers them by email. If you contact us through WhatsApp, that provider’s terms and privacy practices also apply. These services may process technical data and involve international data transfers.

Retention and your rights

Enquiries are retained as needed to respond and, where applicable, for legally required periods. Where provided by applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection by emailing info@nextgenerationtour.es. European data protection rules may also grant the right to complain to the competent authority.

Minors and image rights

This contact form is not intended for children and does not provide consent to publish photographs or videos. Image authorisation for events requires a separate notice and procedure.
